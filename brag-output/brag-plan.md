# Brag Plan: OMNIEL

## What is this app?
OMNIEL is an early-stage AI and technology ecosystem being built from Nigeria; its website presents NOVA, the flagship generalist assistant in development, and lets visitors talk to OMNIEL live by voice.

## The angle
A quiet, confident premium film for a company that insists on honesty: "Intelligence without borders", built from Nigeria. No fake metrics, no hype words — the site's own restraint *is* the brag. The one live, working thing (the voice assistant on the site) is shown doing its job, and NOVA is shown for what it is: the flagship, in development.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark graphite frame. The glass-and-stone intelligence core photograph glows lichen green in the right half. "Intelligence without borders." rises line by line in large Sora type, "without" lit in lichen. Small mono eyebrow above: `OMNIEL / NIGERIA`.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The NOVA workspace card (recreated from the home page's "NOVA Stage"): header `NOVA / Workspace` with a lichen `READY`, the line "Do more, with less unnecessary manual work.", and four capability rows ticking in one by one with lichen square bullets: Voice and text · Computer interaction · Vision and web · Memory and planning.
- The live voice assistant: a cursor presses the "Talk to OMNIEL" capsule, it becomes "Listening…", a caption shows the visitor asking "What is NOVA?", the dot pulses as OMNIEL speaks, and the reply appears as a caption from the real site copy: "NOVA is OMNIEL's generalist assistant: productivity, general assistance and computer interaction."
- The ecosystem list: NOVA (01, flagship) then VYREN, ARVO, KIWI arrive as rows with their real roles and a small status label.

## Outro / punchline
The belief, short: "Small team. Large ambition. Early enough to matter." then the OMNIEL wordmark, `omniel.com.ng`, and the mono line `EARLY-STAGE / PRE-LAUNCH`. The honesty is the button.

## User flow worth showing
Visitor lands → presses "Talk to OMNIEL" → asks "What is NOVA?" → hears (sees) the answer drawn from the live website. This is the one fully working flow on the site and is the centrepiece (Scene 3).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — "a lab in Lagos, lit from inside"
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft crossfades and slides; confidence through restraint; no jokes, no exclamation marks, nothing the site does not itself claim.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21s

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, understated bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (quiet 3s intro, then a steady 120 BPM pulse)
- Music treatment: start at 0s at a low level, gentle fade-in over the hook, sit under everything, fade out over the final 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (`cues/...vol-1...music-cues.md`, ~120 BPM, beats from 3.02s every ~0.5s). Strong cues: 17.02s (outro line lands), 20.02s (wordmark settles). Sequential capability rows (Scene 2): every other beat ≈ 4.02, 5.03, 6.03, 7.02s — each row then holds. Ecosystem rows (Scene 4): ≈ 13.01, 14.02, 15.02, 16.02s, full list held after.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the core photograph's green glow and the voice dot may breathe with the bed; no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse, professional
- Audio-coupled moments: capability row ticks, cursor press on the voice button, a soft chime as the reply caption appears, one clean logo hit at the wordmark
- Restraint rule: no whooshes on every transition, nothing louder than the music, silence-adjacent over busy

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: Intelligence without borders — 3.5s (0.0–3.5)
Dark graphite frame, lab-grid texture at low opacity, the intelligence core photograph on the right with a lichen glow. Mono eyebrow `OMNIEL / NIGERIA`, then "Intelligence / without / borders." rises line by line (Sora, huge, tight tracking); "without" in lichen. Holds ~1.5s settled.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three headline lines rise one after another, then hold together.
Audio intent: quiet, anticipatory; music barely in.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the type breathe).
Music: soft intro.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (the photograph dims and slides left as the workspace card arrives).

### Scene 2 — NOVA, the flagship — 4.5s (3.5–8.0)
Eyebrow `FLAGSHIP SYSTEM / BUILDING NOW`, title "NOVA". Recreated NOVA workspace card on a surface panel: header row `NOVA / WORKSPACE` and lichen `READY`; the statement "Do more, with less unnecessary manual work."; four capability rows arrive one by one with lichen square bullets: Voice and text, Computer interaction, Vision and web, Memory and planning. Small mono footer `IN DEVELOPMENT`. The full card holds ≥1.2s after the last row.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four capability rows arrive one by one (every other beat), then the set holds.
Audio intent: the pulse starts; each row gets a light tick.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card row ticks on the beat grid.
Music: beat entering.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Talk to OMNIEL (the working flow) — 4.5s (8.0–12.5)
Page-like frame: a cursor glides to the bottom-right "Talk to OMNIEL" capsule (off-white pill, graphite text, dot) and presses it. The capsule turns into a glass pill reading "Listening…" with a lichen dot. A visitor caption appears: "What is NOVA?". The dot pulses as OMNIEL answers, and the reply caption appears: "NOVA is OMNIEL's generalist assistant: productivity, general assistance and computer interaction." (holds ≥2s). Small mono tag: `LIVE ON OMNIEL.COM.NG`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated cursor click, state change, question then answer.
Audio intent: intimate; the product responding.
Audio-coupled idea: soft click on the press; a gentle chime when the answer appears.
Music: steady bed.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — One direction — 4.5s (12.5–17.0)
Heading "Different systems. One direction." Rows arrive one by one on a hairline list: `01 NOVA — Generalist AI assistant` (lichen, `FLAGSHIP`), `02 VYREN — Specialist AI for complex technical work`, `03 ARVO — Voice-first AI system`, `04 KIWI — An emerging OMNIEL project`, each with a muted status (`IN DEVELOPMENT` / `EMERGING PROJECT`). Full list holds ≥1.2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four rows, one by one on alternate beats, then held.
Audio intent: building gently toward the close.
Audio-coupled idea: row ticks, quieter than Scene 2.
Music: bed.
Transition mood: clean wipe to the off-white band → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Close — 4.0s (17.0–21.0)
Off-white band (the site's closing-section treatment), graphite type: "Small team. Large ambition. Early enough to matter." lands on the 17.02s strong cue and holds. Then the OMNIEL wordmark (inked for the light band), `omniel.com.ng`, and mono `EARLY-STAGE / PRE-LAUNCH` settle on the 20.02s cue. Music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: line, then wordmark and URL.
Audio intent: resolution; one clean logo hit.
Audio-coupled idea: logo hit on the wordmark.
Music: fade out.
Transition mood: end.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, understated, quietly confident
**Audio summary:** a near-silent open for the headline, a steady pulse carrying NOVA and the live voice demo, and a soft resolve on the wordmark.

Duration check: 3.5 + 4.5 + 4.5 + 4.5 + 4.0 = 21.0s.
